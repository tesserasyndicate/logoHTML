AM SURVEYORSBrand Identity · Concept

Every line, on the ground.

A brand system for a land survey practice in Pune, built from the AM mark: sharp diagonals, true bearings and measured boundaries.

01 · Concept

The line is the product.

Surveying turns land into lines: boundaries, contours, bearings and sections. The AM mark is already built from two crossing diagonals, so the identity treats it as a survey line and builds everything from that language.

Proposed tagline: Every boundary, measured. No claims about experience, accuracy or clients are made.

07 · Rules

Keep the line true.

Mark

Use the supplied mark only. Colour it from the palette. Keep clear space equal to the width of one stroke group.

Graphics

Contours and grids sit behind, never through, the logo. One graphic per layout.

Contrast

Navy text on lime or sky. White on cobalt or navy. Never lime on sky.
